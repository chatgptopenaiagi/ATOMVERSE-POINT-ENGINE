## MISSION 001: THE FIRST ATOMIC POINT
### THE GENESIS OF A CONTROLLABLE COMPUTATIONAL ENTITY

The following foundational vision and final directive are preserved from the human creator's Mission 001 brief. The engineering stages are mapped separately in [MISSION-001.md](../SPECIFICATIONS/MISSION-001.md). This is a computational experiment, not a physical atomic model.

## The original human vision

The human creator proposes a fundamental computational experiment.

Instead of beginning with a large universe containing millions of objects, begin with exactly one controllable point.

This point represents the first elementary computational entity.

Its geometric form is spherical.

It exists inside a three-dimensional coordinate system.

Its location can be chosen freely.

Its movement can be initiated, modified, interrupted, reversed, accelerated, recorded and reproduced.

Its size and color can change.

Its physical interaction rules can change.

It can pass through a wall when configured to do so.

It can collide with a wall when configured differently.

It can remain stationary or move continuously.

Additional points may eventually connect to form straight lines, curved lines, helices and complex spatial structures.

The first objective is not to simulate an entire physical universe.

**THE FIRST OBJECTIVE IS TO ACHIEVE PRECISE, CONTROLLABLE, OBSERVABLE AND REPRODUCIBLE EXISTENCE OF ONE SPHERICAL COMPUTATIONAL POINT.**

Preserve this human concept as the project's foundational specification.

Do not replace it with a generic game engine architecture.

The engine must distinguish the mathematical point from the rendered spherical object.

The sphere must never be mistaken for a physical atom.

It is a controllable computational entity.
